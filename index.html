Texts

  • Ὅμηρος · Homer Iliad 6.146 (generations of leaves); Odyssey 2.1 (“rosy-fingered Dawn”), 7.114 and 7.117–118 (the orchard of Alcinous), 11.12 (the sun set and all the streets grew dark), 11.206–207 (a shadow, a dream).
  • Ὁμηρικοὶ ὕμνοι · Homeric Hymns Hymn 6 to Aphrodite, line 2 (Cyprus).
  • Ἡράκλειτος · Heraclitus the waking and the sleeping (B89, as quoted by Plutarch, De superstitione 166c); the rivers (B49a).
  • Πίνδαρος · Pindar Pythian 8, lines 95–96 (“the dream of a shadow”); Olympian 1, line 1 (“water is best”).
  • Σαπφώ · Sappho fragment 104a, first line (Hesperus); fragment 168B (the moon and the Pleiades).
  • Ἀλκμάν · Alcman fragment 89 (Page), the sleeping mountains.
  • Σιμωνίδης · Simonides “painting is silent poetry”, as quoted by Plutarch.
  • Θουκυδίδης · Thucydides 7.77: “men make the city, not walls or ships empty of men”.
  • Κωνσταντίνος Π. Καβάφης · C. P. Cavafy “Η πόλις” (The City) and “Τείχη” (Walls).

These authors are in the public domain. The English lines are free translations made for this site. The other short Greek lines were written for this project.
